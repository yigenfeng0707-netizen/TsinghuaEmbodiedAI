\section{Physics Audit Report}
\label{app:physics_audit}

This appendix summarizes the trajectory physics audit conducted on
\textbf{2026-08-04} using \code{tools/audit\_trajectory\_physics.py}.
The audit checks each trajectory JSON for:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
    \item \textbf{Object teleport}: per-frame object position jump exceeding
    the \texttt{warn} (0.25\,m) or \texttt{fail} (0.80\,m) threshold.
    \item \textbf{Grasp-base distance}: object-to-base XY distance at grasp
    time, \texttt{warn} 1.6\,m / \texttt{fail} 2.5\,m.
    \item \textbf{Post-grasp teleport}: large jump immediately after grasp
    event (indicates fake attach without contact).
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Note on L2 and L5.}
The worst-jump values for L2 (0.249\,m) and L5 (0.248\,m) are close to the
\texttt{warn} threshold of 0.25\,m but do not exceed it.  Frame-level review
(see Appendix~\ref{app:suspicious_frames}) confirms these are normal
contact-induced displacements on non-grasped objects, not teleport artifacts:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
    \item \textbf{L2 frame 275}: \code{green\_tote\_b01\_lower} shifted
    $+0.234$\,m in Y.  The grasped object was \code{green\_tote\_b01\_upper}
    (frame 196); the lower tote was displaced by contact during upper-tote
    removal.
    \item \textbf{L5 frame 4977}: \code{white\_tote\_b01\_left\_front}
    shifted $-0.232$\,m in Z.  The most recent grasp was
    \code{white\_tote\_b01\_left\_back} (frame 4942); the front tote
    settled downward during placement of the back tote.
\end{itemize}

worst-jump frames (L2 frame 275, L5 frame 4977) identified in
Appendix~\ref{app:physics_audit}.  Replay GIFs were generated on a
DSW GPU instance using the official \code{RobosuiteBackend.replay\_trajectory}
pipeline with \code{MUJOCO\_GL=glx} + Xvfb headless rendering.

The displacement occurs 79 frames after the grasp event (frame 196) and
is dominated by the Y component ($+0.234$\,m), consistent with the lower
tote being pushed when the upper tote is lifted.  The grasped object
(\code{green\_tote\_b01\_upper}) shows no teleport.  Visual inspection of
the birdview GIF (frames 270$\to$275$\to$280) confirms continuous motion
with no discontinuity.

The displacement occurs 35 frames after the most recent grasp (frame 4942)

front tote settling downward when the back tote is placed adjacent to it.
Visual inspection of the birdview GIF (frames 4940$\to$4959$\to$4978)
confirms a gradual downward motion, not a discontinuous teleport.

\paragraph{Conclusion.}
Both anomalous frames are \textbf{normal physical contact displacements} on
non-grasped objects, not teleport or fake-attach violations.  The worst-jump
values (0.249\,m / 0.248\,m) are below the 0.25\,m warn threshold, and the
trajectory physics audit confirms \textbf{fail=0, warn=0, ok=5} for all five
submitted trajectories.
